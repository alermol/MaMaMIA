# MaMaMIA 0.1.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-04

First public release.

MaMaMIA is an interactive dashboard for detecting interspecific introgressions
from Genotyping-by-Sequencing (GBS) coverage. It compares donor and recipient
depth in exact windows, corrects GC bias, masks unreliable regions, segments
with CBS, calibrates calls by segment length, and reports substitutions,
additions, deletions, complex and other events. The method is ploidy-agnostic
and supports any number of donor and recipient components.
